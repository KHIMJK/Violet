Our Mission

We believe beauty is
for everyone

Founded in 2023, Violet Beauty was born from a simple idea: skincare and beauty products should work in harmony with nature and science.

Every formula is dermatologically tested, cruelty-free, vegan, and crafted using premium natural ingredients sourced responsibly from 30+ countries worldwide.

Our mission is to help every person feel confident in their own skin — naturally radiant, healthy, and glowing.

Our Journey

How we got here

2021
The Idea Sparks
Amara Chen walks away from a senior chemist role after realizing most "natural" brands aren't telling the full story. The seed for Violet Beauty is planted.
💡
2022
Two Years in the Lab
Formulating, testing, reformulating. Working with dermatologists and ethical suppliers to get every formula exactly right before a single product ships.
2023
Violet Beauty Launches
Our first 12 products hit the shelves. Within 6 months, we reach 10,000 customers — driven entirely by word of mouth and a commitment that products truly work.
2024
Going Global
Expanded to 30+ countries. Launched Leaping Bunny certification. Our perfume and hair care lines debut, completing the full beauty ritual vision.
2026
50,000+ Happy Customers
And counting. We continue to grow by staying true to our founding promise: science, nature, and honesty in every single drop.
