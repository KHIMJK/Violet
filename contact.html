FAQ

Common Questions

What is your return policy? +
We offer free returns within 30 days of purchase. Products must be unused and in original packaging. Simply email us and we'll arrange a pickup.
How long does shipping take? +
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping (1–2 days) is available at checkout. Free shipping on orders over $60.
Are your products vegan? +
Yes! Every single Violet Beauty product is 100% vegan and cruelty-free. We are certified by PETA and Leaping Bunny.
Can I track my order? +
Absolutely! Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email. You can also track your order in your account dashboard.
Do you ship internationally? +
Yes, we ship to 30+ countries worldwide. International shipping typically takes 7–14 business days. Duties and taxes may apply.
